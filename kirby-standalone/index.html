Rubric in motion

Walk: local foot translation and rotation, with body weight shift.

Body turn: whole-character rotation about a ground-level local Y-axis pivot.

Rod gesture: local arbitrary-axis grip rotation; the arm carries the hand and attached rod.

Hop: vertical translation with nonuniform squash/stretch; the rod stays rigid.

Hierarchy: parent transforms carry attached child parts throughout the sequence, not only in one phase.

Presentation: removable stage and stylized contact shadow; no shared environment or other characters yet.
